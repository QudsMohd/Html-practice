Welcome to the portal

This online system allows registered taxpayers to file returns, manage payments, and track submissions online. The

portal is designed to simplify communication between taxpayers and the tax authority.

Use the navigation menu above to explore filing options, review your payment history, or contact support if you need

assistance.New users must verify their email before submitting a first return

Filing types include VAT, CIT, and PIT.
